# Balance audit

Generated by `pnpm sim:audit` (web/sim/audit.sim.ts) from the game rules in `web/src/game`. Do not edit by hand: change `web/sim/scenarios.ts` or `web/sim/lab.ts` and run it again. What the numbers mean for the game is argued in [BALANCE_REVIEW.md](BALANCE_REVIEW.md).

## 0. Read this first: simulation, not measurement

**Every table in this file is a simulation with model players.** No table here is measured from people. The only real data that exists today is listed in BALANCE_REVIEW.md (the on-chain history of the testnet deployment) and whatever the in-game telemetry has collected in a browser (`#/stats`, see web/README.md).

- **Two rule sets.** *On the contracts* (production) the contract rolls the fish and its quality; the hook and the fight decide only how long it takes, because a missed hook or a lost fight is another try (three at most, each easier). *Local game* (`VITE_GAME_MODE=local`) is the browser-only game, where a missed hook or a lost fight loses the fish and quality is how well it was fought. Each table says which it shows.
- **PEARL is in the client's units**, 10× the contract's (a Roach is 9 here and 0.9 on the contract). Energy is the contract's: a bar of 20, one point per 30 minutes, 48 a day at most. The local game regenerates a point a minute; that is not what is simulated.
- **Model players** (assumptions, see `PLAYERS` in web/sim/lab.ts). The fight model is the game's own `simulateFight`; each cell is 1,500 fights.

| Model player | Fight: reaction | Fight: aim error | Fight: warnings missed | Hook: median reaction | Hook: spread (log-sd) | Hook: not watching | Seconds on the catch card |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Beginner | 0.32 s | 0.08 | 20% | 0.6 s | 0.4 | 5% | 5 |
| Average | 0.25 s | 0.06 | 10% | 0.45 s | 0.32 | 2% | 3.5 |
| Experienced | 0.18 s | 0.04 | 4% | 0.33 s | 0.25 | 1% | 2.5 |

- **Time** per cast is the client's own timings (cast animation 0.9 s, wait for a bite 2–5 s or 3–7 s in rough water, the fight itself) plus, on the contracts, an assumed 4 s for the cast transaction and 4 s for the settlement. The transaction times are a guess, not a measurement.
- **The economy player** sells every fish at once (so every sale has the +20% fresh bonus), keeps one specimen of each special fish, claims rewards, repairs, buys rods in price order as soon as it can pay, and fishes wherever the net PEARL per energy is best with what it owns. Real players are less efficient; read PEARL figures as an upper bound for a given amount of energy.
